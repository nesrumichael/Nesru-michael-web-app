## 🚀 Key Platform Capabilities
1. **Modern Cosmic & Glassmorphic Landing Experience:** Opens with live orbital telemetry, instant multi-language switching, and Sign Up / Sign In authentication.
2. **Unlimited Followers & 1,000-Friend Circle Engine:**
   - **Followers:** Unlimited (`∞`) scaling across the global network.
   - **Friends:** Strictly enforced 1,000-friend maximum cap (`MAX_FRIENDS = 1000`) for meaningful two-way connections.
3. **Instant Worldwide Post Broadcast:** Posts created by any user broadcast instantaneously via WebSockets (`Socket.IO`) to all connected users worldwide and are encapsulated with LEO/GEO Satellite Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN) packet metadata.
4. **Real-Time Private Friend Chat:** Encrypted WebSocket direct messaging between connected friends.
5. **Global Country Codes & World Languages:** Built-in support for 195+ country dialing codes (including `+251` Ethiopia and `+8816` Orbital Space Station) and major world languages (`Afaan Oromoo`, `English`, `Amharic`, `Arabic`, `French`, `Spanish`, `Chinese`, `Hindi`, `Swahili`, etc.).
6. **Complete Governance Suite:** Includes dedicated **About**, **Terms of Transparency**, **Privacy & Policy**, and **Support & Contact** portals.
